The Awkward Dinner Party 🍷

Welcome to The Awkward Dinner Party, an interactive scenario game for English language learners (B1–B2 level). You have been invited to a formal dinner at the home of your new department manager, Ms. Clara Vance. You want to make a great impression, but social landmines, unexpected dish ingredients, political debates, and household mishaps await!

How to Play & Score:
  • 10 Sequential Scenarios: Navigate 10 chronological dinner moments smoothly.
  • 4 Options Per Scenario: Choices appear in a randomized order every play!
  • 🟩 Excellent Response (+2 pts): Culturally fluent, tactful, and articulate.
  • 🟦 Good Response (+1 pt): Polite and functional, but less diplomatic.
  • 🟧 Average Response (+0 pts): Neutral; safe but passive or slightly awkward.
  • 🟥 Bad Response (-1 pt): Rude, offensive, or chaotic. Subtracts 1 point and forces a retry!
  • Maximum Score: 20 Points. View your performance band at the end!
