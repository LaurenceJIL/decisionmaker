The Awkward Dinner Party 🍷

Welcome to The Awkward Dinner Party! This is an interactive game for English learners (A2 level). Your manager, Ms. Vance, invites you to dinner at her house. You want to be polite and make a good impression, but small social problems keep happening!

Game Rules & Points:
  • 10 Scenarios: Make a polite choice at each moment of the dinner.
  • 4 Options Every Time: The answers appear in a random order every game!
  • 🟩 Excellent Answer (+2 points): Very polite and friendly!
  • 🟦 Good Answer (+1 point): A polite choice.
  • 🟧 Average Answer (+0 points): Okay, but a little quiet or awkward.
  • 🟥 Bad Answer (-1 point): Rude or impolite. You lose 1 point and must try again!
  • Maximum Points: 20 Points.
