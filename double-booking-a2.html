The Double Booking 📅

Welcome to The Double Booking! This is an interactive game for English learners (A2 level). It is Friday at 6:00 PM. You suddenly realize you have made two plans at 7:00 PM: a farewell dinner for your best friend Marco, and an important work dinner with your boss, Sarah, and new clients!

Game Rules & Points:
  • 10 Scenarios: Make a smart choice at each moment of your evening.
  • 4 Options Every Time: The answers appear in a random order every game!
  • 🟩 Excellent Answer (+2 points): Very smart, honest, and polite!
  • 🟦 Good Answer (+1 point): A good and helpful choice.
  • 🟧 Average Answer (+0 points): Okay, but slow or a bit risky.
  • 🟥 Bad Answer (-1 point): Rude, dishonest, or bad. You lose 1 point and must try again!
  • Maximum Points: 20 Points.
