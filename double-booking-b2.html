The Double Booking 📅

Welcome to The Double Booking, an interactive scenario game for English language learners (B1–B2 level). It is Friday evening at 6:00 PM. You suddenly realize you have made two conflicting commitments at 7:00 PM on opposite sides of town: a farewell dinner for your best friend Marco, and an important networking dinner with potential high-value clients hosted by your boss, Sarah.

How to Play & Score:
  • 10 Sequential Scenarios: Handle the scheduling crisis chronologically.
  • 4 Options Per Scenario: Choices appear in a randomized order every play!
  • 🟩 Excellent Response (+2 pts): Professional, diplomatic, honest, and tactful.
  • 🟦 Good Response (+1 pt): Reasonable and polite, but less ideal.
  • 🟧 Average Response (+0 pts): Neutral; safe but passive, vague, or risky.
  • 🟥 Bad Response (-1 pt): Rude, dishonest, or chaotic. Subtracts 1 point and forces a retry!
  • Maximum Score: 20 Points. View your performance band at the end!
