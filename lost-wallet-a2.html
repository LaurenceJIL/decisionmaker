The Lost Wallet 👝

Welcome to The Lost Wallet! This is an interactive game for English learners (A2 level). You find a lost wallet in a busy café. You have 10 choices to make to help return it to its owner before her train leaves!

Game Rules & Points:
  • 10 Scenarios: Make a choice at each step of the story.
  • 4 Options Every Time: The answers appear in a random order every game!
  • 🟩 Excellent Answer (+2 points): Very polite, fast, and helpful!
  • 🟦 Good Answer (+1 point): A good and helpful choice.
  • 🟧 Average Answer (+0 points): Okay, but slow or not very helpful.
  • 🟥 Bad Answer (-1 point): Rude or wrong. You lose 1 point and must try again!
  • Maximum Points: 20 Points.
