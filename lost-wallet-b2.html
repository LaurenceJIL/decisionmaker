The Lost Wallet 👝

Welcome to The Lost Wallet, an interactive story game designed for English language learners (B1–B2 level). You will navigate a 10-scenario real-world emergency that tests your communication skills, problem-solving, and social judgment.

How to Play & Score:
  • 10 Sequential Scenarios: Make chronological decisions under time pressure.
  • 4 Options Per Scenario: Choices are randomized each time you play!
  • 🟩 Excellent Response (+2 pts): Proactive, polite, and effective.
  • 🟦 Good Response (+1 pt): Functional and reasonable.
  • 🟧 Average Response (+0 pts): Neutral; safe but slow or indirect.
  • 🟥 Bad Response (-1 pt): Rude or harmful. Subtracts 1 point and forces you to try again.
  • Maximum Score: 20 Points. Complete all scenarios to view your feedback band!
