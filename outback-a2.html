G'day, Adventurer! Welcome to the Outback.

Are you ready for a big drive in the Australian desert? You will drive a big 4WD car for 2,000 kilometres. You will travel on sandy tracks, cross rivers, and drive over big sand dunes from Alice Springs to Birdsville.

This game helps you practice your English reading (A2 Level) and tests your survival skills!

  • 12+ Decisions: Choose good options for water, petrol, tyres, and routes.
  • Dynamic Routes: Average choices give you a second chance. Bad choices lose points!
  • Emergency Checkpoints: Be careful! Bad emergency choices end the game.
  • Australian English: Learn fun Australian words like track, ute, tucker, and tyres.

Pack your bags, check your water, and let's go into the wild Outback!
