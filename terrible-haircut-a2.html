The Terrible Haircut ✂️

Welcome to The Terrible Haircut! This is an interactive scenario game for English learners (A2–B1 level). It is 3:00 PM on Friday. You have an important job interview at 5:00 PM today. You are at a trendy hair salon, but when the hairdresser turns your chair around... your hair looks completely terrible!

Game Rules & Points:
  • 10 Scenarios: Make polite, smart decisions to save your haircut and job interview.
  • 4 Options Every Time: Choices are randomized every time you play!
  • 🟩 Excellent Answer (+2 points): Polite, honest, and smart!
  • 🟦 Good Answer (+1 point): Helpful and reasonable.
  • 🟧 Average Answer (+0 points): Okay, but slow or passive.
  • 🟥 Bad Answer (-1 point): Angry, rude, or unhelpful. You lose 1 point and must try again!
  • Maximum Points: 20 Points.
