The Terrible Haircut ✂️

Welcome to The Terrible Haircut, an interactive scenario game for English language learners (B1–B2 level). It is Friday afternoon at 3:00 PM. You have a high-stakes executive job interview at 5:00 PM. You visit a high-end salon for a quick trim, but when the stylist swivels your chair toward the mirror, you are greeted by an absolute aesthetic disaster!

How to Play & Score:
  • 10 Sequential Scenarios: Navigate a cosmetic crisis under tight time constraints.
  • 4 Options Per Scenario: Choices appear in a randomized order every play!
  • 🟩 Excellent Response (+2 pts): Assertive, diplomatic, resourceful, and polished.
  • 🟦 Good Response (+1 pt): Reasonable and polite, though less effective.
  • 🟧 Average Response (+0 pts): Neutral; safe but passive, timid, or delayed.
  • 🟥 Bad Response (-1 pt): Rude, hysterical, or chaotic. Subtracts 1 point and forces a retry!
  • Maximum Score: 20 Points. View your performance band at the end!
